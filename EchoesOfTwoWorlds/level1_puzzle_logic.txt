ECHOES OF TWO WORLDS — LEVEL 1 PUZZLE LOGIC

Level: Garden of Lost Memories

Objective:
Discover three runes and restore the Memory Seal.

Rune order:
1. Moon Rune
2. Sun Rune
3. Star Rune

Controls:
- E: Interact with a rune or portal
- J: Open the Clue Log
- ESC: Close the Clue Log

Clue collection:
Interacting with each rune displays its clue and records it in the Clue Log.

Puzzle rules:
- Accept Moon as the first activation.
- Accept Sun as the second activation.
- Accept Star as the third activation.
- If the player activates a rune out of order, display an error message and reset the activation sequence.

Victory condition:
After all three runes are activated in the correct order, restore the Memory Seal and open the portal.

Completion:
When the player presses E near the open portal, display level1_complete.png.

Required assets:
- level1_puzzle.png
- level1_journal.png
- level1_complete.png

Note: All keyboard controls, puzzle states, and portal transitions must be implemented in Unity.
