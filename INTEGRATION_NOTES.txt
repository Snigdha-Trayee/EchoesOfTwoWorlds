Run
1. Extract the complete ZIP to a new folder.
2. Open that folder in VS Code.
3. In the terminal run: py -3.13 main.py

Controls
- WASD or arrow keys: move
- E: collect a memory / interact with portal or mechanism
- SPACE: attack the Shadow Guardian in the Observatory
- 1, 2, 3: choose runes while the puzzle is open
- ENTER: submit puzzle; BACKSPACE: clear; ESC: close puzzle/quit
- R: restart after victory or game over
