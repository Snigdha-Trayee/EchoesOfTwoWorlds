# Echoes of Two Worlds — Game Design Document

## Game Overview
Genre: Fantasy adventure and puzzle exploration.

The player explores two connected worlds, discovers clues and three ancient symbols, defeats a guardian, unlocks an ancient arch, and enters a portal to reach the Observatory.

## Story
An ancient passage between two worlds has been sealed. The player must explore forgotten ruins, uncover clues, discover three symbols, and defeat the guardian protecting the passage.

## Level 1 — The Forgotten Garden
Landmarks:
- Starting Point
- Ancient Garden
- Inscription Stone
- Symbol Chamber
- Guardian Arena
- Ancient Arch

Objectives:
1. Explore the garden.
2. Discover an ancient clue.
3. Find all three ancient symbols.
4. Defeat the guardian.
5. Unlock the Ancient Arch.

## Level 2 — The Observatory
Proposed landmarks:
- Portal Arrival Point
- Observatory Entrance
- Star Map Hall
- Celestial Puzzle Chamber
- Final Discovery Point

Proposed objectives:
1. Enter the Observatory.
2. Explore the environment.
3. Investigate landmarks and clues.
4. Complete the final discovery objective.

## Progression Rules
- Discover at least one clue.
- Find all three symbols.
- Defeat the guardian.
- Unlock the arch only after its prerequisites are met.
- Enter the portal after unlocking the arch.

## Integration Notes
The Python module manages objective progression, discoveries, and story events. Unity integration is needed for player movement, collisions, map geometry, UI, enemies, and scene transitions.

## Design Status
This is a proposed design. Exact map coordinates, player routes, and clue positions must be confirmed with the team.
